How to read a match card

One card per match, one film per matchday, in kick-off order. Every column is split at the halfway line: the home team on top, the away team below.

  • The line-ups: both elevens by formation, each half in its club colour. On a player: his goals (white chip), assists (outlined), a card on the photo, and — when he went off — who came on and when, with what that substitute did. The big numbers are the score; the corner labels give each club's formation and its table place before → after.
  • Every shot: each attempt in the zone it was taken from (the match commentary names the zone, not the exact spot) — a ring = goal, a dot = on target, a circle = off target, a cross = blocked.
  • The flow, across the halfway line: who pushed in every 5 minutes (shots, shots on target, corners, goals), home up, away down; the centre line takes the colour of whoever is ahead.
  • On goal: where each side's attempts went — goals, saves, and misses wide or over. The numbers across the middle: home up, away down, the lower value greyed.

Credits

Line-ups, goals, cards, substitutions, team stats and the attempts from ESPN match reports and their commentary, checked every update: ESPN's score must equal the result in the Season Tower (football-data.org) and the goals in the report must add up to it. Table places from the official standings. Player photos are the official season portraits published by the leagues. Club crests via TheSportsDB; league logos from the leagues.
