How to read a squad card

One card per player of the club, most league minutes first. The big number on the photo is his shirt number; the club name in the top bar goes back to its team card.

  • Outfield players have the scorer card. The highlighted number is his goals if he has scored, his minutes if not; then goals or minutes, assists, games (and starts), cards and, in Serie A, the average Fantacalcio mark.
  • The season: one box per club league game, coloured by the result — dark green won by 2+, green won by 1, yellow draw, grey 0-0, red lost by 1, dark red lost by 2+. His minutes fill the box from the bottom (a starter) or the top (off the bench); a pale box is a game he was on the bench and not used, a hatched one a game he was not in the matchday squad (red stripes: suspended, the game after a red card — the only reason ever shown); above it ● a goal, ○ an assist; under it the opponent and, in Serie A, his Fantacalcio mark. Click a box for the match card.
  • Goalkeepers have the goalkeeper card: saves in each box with the goals against him above, every shot on target he faced on one goal, and the goals he conceded.
  • The head coach follows the last player with league minutes: his points per game and record, the season strip with the games before him greyed and tagged with the coach then, every coach the club has had this season (from FotMob's line-ups) and his substitutions: who he sends on, who he takes off, the change he repeats and each recent game's changes. In Serie A, his Fantacalcio mark sits under each of his games.
  • Minutes, goals, assists, cards and saves come from the match reports of this season's games.
