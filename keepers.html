How to read a goalkeeper card

One card per keeper, ranked by saves (ties share a rank). All Leagues is the top 25 across the five leagues; each league logo lists every club's first-choice keeper and anyone with three or more starts.

  • The season: one box per league game of his club — coloured by the result, his saves in it, a red ring above for every goal he conceded. Pale boxes are games he didn't start; empty outlines are still to play.
  • Every shot on target he faced, on one goal as the shooter saw it: a ring = saved, a red dot = goal. Each of the six zones says how many he saved of those he faced there.
  • The goals against him: minute, scorer, how (header, penalty, own goal) and the match. Hover or tap a game to light up its goals.
  • Saves are ESPN's count when he played the whole game, else the commentary's while he was on; save % = saves ÷ (saves + goals conceded); a clean sheet needs the full game.
  • Order by (the button after the keeper selector, bottom): saves (the default), save % or clean sheets (best first; under three games last) or age (youngest first) — the same keepers, reordered; the number it orders by is in the club colour on each card. The choice is remembered.
  • Season | Tactical | Heatmaps (the switch by his club, or t to step through): Tactical shows every game of his club — opponent and score, where he lined up in the XI (a keeper: the shots on target he faced and his saves), his role and the formation, minutes, goals, assists, cards and, in Serie A, the Fantacalcio mark. Heatmaps keeps the same boxes but draws, on each pitch, where he touched the ball in that game, always attacking up (FotMob), and his shots in the zone they came from (● goal · ○ on target · × off · ▪ blocked); a keeper's pitch is the 36 m in front of his goal, with every shot he faced and, in red, the goals he let in. In Serie A and the Premier League the minutes come with the km he ran (hover a game for his top speed). A box opens its match card; the choice holds for every card.
