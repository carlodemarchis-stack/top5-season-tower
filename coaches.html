How to read a coach card

One card per coach, ranked by points per game in his league games at the club this season (a coach with fewer than three games ranks after the rest). Every club's current head coach has a card; so does a coach who left during the season after three or more games (former). All Leagues ranks all five leagues on one scale.

  • The numbers: points per game, W-D-L, goals for–against, clean sheets and his bookings (Serie A from the league's own line-ups, elsewhere from FotMob, which misses some); in Serie A his average Fantacalcio mark. The club's place, points and goal difference are on the line under his name.
  • Season | Tactical | Subs (the switch by the club, or t): Tactical shows every club game with his starting XI on the pitch (shirt numbers where there is room, names on hover), ↓ on each player he took off, then the formation, his first change (⇄), his bookings and, in Serie A, his mark. Hover a game for all his changes, a player's dot to see him alone in every game with a card of his game; a box opens its match card.
  • The season: one box per club league game, coloured by the result, the formation over it. Greyed boxes are games another coach was on the bench for, tagged under the box (a stand-in, or the coach before or after him).
  • The bench this season: every coach the club has had, with his record.
  • Subs: how many changes, when the first one comes, who he sends on and takes off, the change he repeats; when in the game he makes them (by 15 minutes) and the score at the time (ahead, level, behind); and every game's changes, minute by minute (● a goal, ○ an assist by the sub).
  • Who was on the bench comes from each match's line-up (FotMob), so a sacking or a stand-in shows from the right game on.
