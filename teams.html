How to read a team card

One card per club, in table order. The photo panel shows the club's three top scorers; the big number is its points.

  • The season: one box per league game — matchdays 1–19 on the top row, 20–38 below (1–17 / 18–34 in Ligue 1 and the Bundesliga) — coloured by the result: dark green won by 2+, green won by 1, yellow draw, grey 0-0, red lost by 1, dark red lost by 2+. The darker part of each box is the club's share of possession. Empty outlines are games still to play.
  • When they score: every goal on a 0–90' line — scored above the line, conceded below (red rings); dashed = own goal.
  • Position: where the club stood in the table after each matchday.
  • Scorers: goals (and assists). Hover or tap a scorer or a match to light up the goals.
  • Squad tab (t): the whole squad as player cards by position, most minutes first — minutes, goals and assists (saves and goals conceded for keepers), shirt number behind the photo; a red + marks an injury, a card colour a booking, faded players haven't played yet.
