How to read a card

One card per player: the top 25 goalscorers across Serie A, the Premier League, LaLiga, Ligue 1 and the Bundesliga, plus everyone tied with the 25th. Ordered by goals; players level on goals share a rank.

  • Goals in the club colour, with penalties; then assists, games (and starts), minutes, minutes per goal, shots on target / shots.
  • Match by match: one box per club league game, in matchday order, coloured by the club's result — dark green won by 2+, green won by 1, yellow draw, grey 0-0, red lost by 1, dark red lost by 2+. Above the box: ● a goal (P = penalty), ○ an assist. Below: the opponent (@ = away), a bar for the minutes he played, “sub” if he came off the bench, “DNP” if he did not play.
  • The goals: minute, opponent, PEN / HEAD / FK, and who made the assist.
  • The league logos in the top bar jump to that league's first scorer; the number beside each is how many of its players made the list.
