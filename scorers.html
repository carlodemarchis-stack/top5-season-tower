How to read a card

One card per player. Top 5 is the top 50 goalscorers across Serie A, the Premier League, LaLiga, Ligue 1 and the Bundesliga; each league logo opens that league's own top 25. Everyone tied at the cut is included (a league's list never goes below 2 goals). Ordered by goals; players level on goals share a rank.

  • Goals in the club colour, with penalties; then assists, games (and starts), minutes, minutes per goal, shots on target / shots.
  • The season: one box per club league game — matchdays 1–19 on the top row, 20–38 below (1–17 / 18–34 in Ligue 1 and the Bundesliga) — coloured by the club's result: dark green won by 2+, green won by 1, yellow draw, grey 0-0, red lost by 1, dark red lost by 2+. Under each box, the opponent (@ = away). Empty outlines are games still to play; a white box with a grey outline is a game he missed.
  • Minutes fill the box from the bottom up, as the 90 minutes run: a starter's darker part sits at the bottom, a substitute's at the top (he came on for the end); a solid box is a full game. Above the box: ● a goal (ringed = penalty), ○ an assist.
  • When he scores: every goal on a 0–90' line — ● goal, ringed = penalty, ■ header, dashed = free kick — with how many came in each half and how many after coming off the bench. Hover or tap a match and its goals light up in the club colour here and in the goal list.
  • The goals: minute, opponent, PEN / HEAD / FK, and who made the assist.
  • The top bar switches film: Top 5 or one league. The number beside each is how many players that film has.
